# Undergraduate conference grants — research notes

Researched 19 September 2026 for scholarshipx. Starting list: [BadgeUp](https://github.com/Jose-Gael-Cruz-Lopez/BadgeUp) (README snapshot from `main`). Extra sources: official conference / society pages only.

**Include:** a sponsor-owned grant, scholarship, travel award, diversity scholarship, student-volunteer waived fee, or financial-aid program.  
**Skip:** student-priced tickets with no aid program, free virtual passes, academic tuition scholarships that do not fund conference attendance, and programs with no official apply page.

Deadlines move. Confirm on the official page before applying. Several 2026 cycles are already closed as of this date; they stay in the list because the program is recurring and the apply URL is the sponsor’s own.

---

## Method

- Pulled the full BadgeUp README from [github.com/Jose-Gael-Cruz-Lopez/BadgeUp](https://github.com/Jose-Gael-Cruz-Lopez/BadgeUp).
- Kept events BadgeUp tagged Scholarship / Travel Scholarship / Travel Award / Free (apply-to-attend with funding).
- Added well-known CS/STEM/women/first-gen programs missing from BadgeUp (WiCyS, USENIX, AAAI, ERN, ICML, ICLR, DjangoCon, SIGGRAPH SV, ACS WCC, AMS).
- Verified each grant URL against the organizer’s domain (HTTP 200, or official content confirmed via the society site when the host returns bot 403).
- Dropped events that only offer a student ticket.
